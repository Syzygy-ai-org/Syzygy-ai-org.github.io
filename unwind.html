Unwind

A quiet corner of SYZYGY.

Work is heavy sometimes. This isn't a productivity hack — it's a small set of free tools for breathing, reflecting, creating, and recharging, built around techniques people actually use for stress and burnout.

Why SYZYGY built this: we spend most of this site helping people work more efficiently with AI. But faster work isn't the same as sustainable work. These tools exist because we think caring about how people feel — not just how much they get done — is part of doing this well.
