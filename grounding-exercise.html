5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

A guided sensory grounding exercise used in CBT and DBT to bring racing thoughts back to the present moment.

Why this helps: The 5-4-3-2-1 technique is a sensory grounding exercise used in cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and dialectical behavior therapy (DBT) to interrupt anxious or racing thoughts by anchoring your attention in the present moment through your senses.
