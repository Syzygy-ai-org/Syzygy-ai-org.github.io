Box Breathing

A slow, structured breathing pattern used in stress-management training to calm the nervous system in under two minutes. No sign-up, nothing saved.

Why this helps: Box breathing — inhale, hold, exhale, hold, each for a slow count of four — is a technique used in stress-management training and cognitive behavioral therapy to help calm the nervous system. It works by slowing your breathing rate, which signals your body to shift out of "fight or flight" mode.
